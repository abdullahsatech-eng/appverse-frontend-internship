Built around what small teams actually need

These are the intended benefits of the product idea. They are design principles, not claims about existing customers or results.

  • Plain language first

    Guidance written for owners and managers, not only for security specialists.

  • Prioritised, not overwhelming

    A short ordered list of next steps instead of an endless wall of findings.

  • Honest about its limits

    A readiness guide helps you organise work. It never replaces a professional assessment.

  • Evidence in one place

    Policies, checklists and notes kept together so progress is easy to explain.

What this page is not: there are no customer logos, testimonials, certifications or statistics here because none exist. Every number in the previews is illustrative sample data.

Proposed capabilities

Six conceptual features that would help a small team move from "we hope we are fine" to "we know our next three steps".

  • Security readiness overview

    One summary of how your current practices compare with a simple readiness checklist, grouped by area.

  • Prioritised improvement roadmap

    Recommended actions ordered by effort and impact so the most useful work comes first.

  • Risk and issue organisation

    Group open concerns by category, owner and urgency instead of tracking them in scattered notes.

  • Evidence and policy checklist

    Keep a record of which policies exist and what supports them, ready to share with a client or partner.

  • Team security awareness

    Short, practical reminders and a simple way to see who has completed which awareness activity.

  • Progress visibility

    See readiness change over time so improvement is visible to the whole team, not just one person.

How it would work

A simple three-step concept. This static page demonstrates the idea; it does not perform these steps itself.

  1. 1

    Understand your current practices

    Answer a short, plain-language questionnaire about accounts, devices, backups and policies to describe where you are today.

  2. 2

    Identify and prioritise improvements

    Receive a short, ordered list of suggested actions, grouped by area and ranked by effort and likely benefit.

  3. 3

    Track progress toward readiness

    Mark work as done, attach supporting evidence and watch your readiness picture improve over time.

Who it could help

Four example situations where a lightweight readiness workspace could be useful.

  • Small businesses

    An owner without an IT department could see which basic protections are in place and what to ask a provider to do.

  • Online stores

    A shop could keep track of account security, backups and staff habits that protect customer trust.

  • Software agencies

    An agency could organise its own practices and show clients a clear record of the improvements it is making.

  • Startups

    A young team could build good habits early and have tidy answers ready when partners ask about security.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about what this concept is, and what it is not.

Is ReadySignal a real product?

No. ReadySignal is a concept created as a frontend internship project. This page is a static HTML and CSS design that illustrates how the product could look and be explained.

Who is the concept designed for?

Small and medium-sized businesses, online stores, small software agencies and startups that do not have a dedicated security team.

How would onboarding work?

The idea is a short questionnaire about your current practices, followed by a first roadmap of suggested actions. This page does not collect any answers or personal data.

Would it scan or monitor my systems?

No. The concept is a readiness guide that helps you organise and track work. It would not perform vulnerability scanning, threat detection or live monitoring, and it would not guarantee that you are secure.

Does it provide compliance certification?

No. It could help you collect evidence and organise policies, but it would not certify compliance with any standard or replace an independent audit.

Are the prices real?

No. The three tiers are sample prices used to demonstrate a pricing layout. There is no checkout, billing or sign-up on this page.
